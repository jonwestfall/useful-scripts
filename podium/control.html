            How this works
            

            Open check-in, then Show on screen: the projector
            shows a QR code and a six-digit code that change every 15 seconds,
            so a photo texted to someone outside stops working almost at once.
            Students scan or type it on their own phone, find their name on the
            course roster (or sign in as a guest) and get a receipt.
            Two people checked in from the same phone are both recorded and
            flagged ⚑ for you to look at; a weaker ⚐ means a couple of
            check-ins came from one network address within a minute or two.
            Anyone without a phone, you mark by hand from the list. Closing
            check-in and opening it again in the same lecture keeps one record.
