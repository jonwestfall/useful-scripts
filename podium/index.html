PODIUM

Free, encrypted, zero-build classroom control

Drive the projector from your iPad.
Never touch the classroom PC again.

Open one browser tab on the machine plugged into the projector and walk away. Slides, PDFs, video, YouTube, background music, handwriting, QR codes, timers and your phone's camera — all of it controlled from a tablet or phone in your hand, from anywhere in the room.

AES-256, encrypted end to end

Built for the room you're actually teaching in

🎓 The everyday lecture

Freeze the projector to flip ahead or cue a video while the room stays on the current slide. TAKE cuts it live the instant you're ready. A pacing clock keeps you honest about the time you have left.

🤝 Handing off to a guest

Pair a device with a QR code in seconds. A substitute or guest lecturer gets a controller that already knows the room — no accounts, no install, nothing to configure before class starts.

🗳️ An interactive session

Run a live poll students answer on their own phones, results private until you reveal them to the room. Point a laser, split the screen, switch between a deck and a document camera without missing a beat.

🖥️ Running your own server

Self-host the relay and Podium gains accounts, a shared slide library, durable session history with every stroke of ink, and course-level access control — while every page keeps working with no server at all.
