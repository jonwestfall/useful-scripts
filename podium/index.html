Drive the projector from your iPad. Open the display on the machine plugged into the projector, then open the controller on your tablet or phone.

Display on the classroom PC — bookmark it. Controller on the iPad: open it and use Share → Add to Home Screen so it launches without Safari's chrome. Planning is the office page: build a lecture's running order, timers and media, save it as a plan file, and load that on the iPad before class.
